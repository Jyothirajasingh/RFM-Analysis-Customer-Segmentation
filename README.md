# RFM Analysis – Customer Segmentation

## Project Overview
This project uses Python to analyse historical online retail transactions, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and explore customer segmentation using the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework.

The objective is to generate actionable business insights that can support customer retention, product performance analysis, inventory planning, and data-driven marketing strategies.

## Business Objectives
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour and revenue contribution.
- Identify high-value and recurring customers.
- Understand product sales trends and seasonal demand.
- Evaluate product sales consistency.
- Apply RFM analysis to explore customer segmentation.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Methodology
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
3. Product and revenue performance analysis
4. Customer revenue analysis across two periods
5. Seasonal sales trend analysis
6. Coefficient of Variation (CV) analysis for product sales consistency
7. RFM metric calculation and customer segmentation

## Key Findings

- **Customer Loyalty:** 63 customers appeared in the top 100 revenue-generating customers in both 2009–2010 and 2010–2011, indicating a recurring base of high-revenue customers.
- **Seasonal Demand:** The *White Hanging Heart T-Light Holder* was the highest-selling product by quantity during December–January in both periods. It also ranked among the top five products during May–June.
- **Highest Monthly Sales Volume:** The *Black and White Paisley Flower Mug* recorded 19,248 units sold in February 2010, the highest quantity for a product in a single month.
- **Revenue Performance:** Stock Code 22423 recorded the highest average monthly revenue in the 2010–2011 dataset, at 10,220.80 in the dataset's currency units.
- **Sales Consistency:** Coefficient of Variation analysis identified products with relatively stable monthly revenue patterns, highlighting opportunities for more predictable inventory planning.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty programmes and personalised engagement campaigns for recurring high-revenue customers.
2. **Seasonal Inventory Planning:** Use historical seasonal demand patterns to forecast stock requirements and reduce stockouts during peak periods.
3. **Product Promotion:** Investigate the factors behind high-volume product sales and explore promotional campaigns and product bundling opportunities.
4. **Revenue Optimisation:** Monitor high-revenue products and prioritise their availability and performance in inventory planning.
5. **Inventory Management:** Use sales consistency analysis to identify products suitable for regular replenishment and better stock management.
6. **Targeted Marketing:** Use validated RFM customer segments to design differentiated campaigns for valuable, occasional, and inactive customers.
